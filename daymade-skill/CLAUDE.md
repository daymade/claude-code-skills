# daymade-skill Suite

## Verification Routing

Before selecting evidence or judging task completion, enter
[skill-creator](skill-creator/SKILL.md). Existing-Skill workflow moves and renames
enter [migration preservation](skill-creator/references/existing-skill-migration.md).
For task-result acceptance examples, use
[named task-result examples](skill-creator/references/change-verification.md#named-task-result-examples).

## Publication Routing

For candidate preparation, publication conflicts and review binding, follow
[release readiness](skill-creator/references/release-readiness.md).

## Supersede Hook (on-demand, never static)

Keep the suite free of static `hooks/hooks.json`. For coexistence detection and the
opt-in supersede kit, follow
[Skill precedence and coexistence](skill-creator/references/skill-precedence-and-coexistence.md).

## Governance Principles

For source, installation, catalog-visibility or cache drift, enter
[skill-governance](skill-governance/SKILL.md) and follow its request router.
For a new or changed Skill's requested local availability, follow the
[source-backed activation and consumed-bundle workflow](skill-governance/references/skill-surface-governance.md#5-reconcile-source-backed-activation)
and the
[fresh-host delivery gate](skill-governance/references/skill-surface-governance.md#14-verify-a-newly-registered-skill).
