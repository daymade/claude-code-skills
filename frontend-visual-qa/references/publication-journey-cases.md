# Publication journey calibration cases

Use these cases when auditing a publication's composition or density,
especially after local checks passed but the user still rejects the whole path.
The executing auditor applies the existing **Information necessity before layout**
check to the declared discovery → selection → reading path. A local line-break
request alone does not authorize a whole-site redesign. Stop at that scope.

The synthetic packets and anonymized observed case below are calibration inputs,
not evidence of a current render or approval of a future site's copy.
In a live audit, obtain the canonical render and perform the journey before
claiming any appearance, interaction or reading result. No script enforces the
semantic judgments below; an auditor must examine the actual task and evidence.

## Failing case: all links work, but the reading routes are interchangeable

Actor: a reader choosing a paper about how candidate changes are selected,
then finding its supporting source and a relevant follow-up.

Supplied observations:

- The home page offers a featured story, a chronological library, a topic
  section and repeated publication-introduction bands with equal visual weight.
- The topic section contains exactly the same stories, ordering and summaries
  as the chronological library. Topic names give no selection criterion.
- Every story row repeats a topic badge, a summary, an expanded-summary preview,
  source metadata and a second identical read action.
- The reader page opens with a large repeat of the cover and publication
  introduction before the argument. Follow-up links say only “Read more.”
- Links, search, source access and figure dialogs work; local overflow, line
  breaks and button states have already passed checks.

Expected audit action: do not issue an overall pass from those local checks.
Inspect the competing discovery routes, row information and article entry as
one task. Cite the duplicate topic ordering and missing selection criterion as
specific evidence of a route that adds no topic-based choice. Identify which
context can be disclosed or relocated without losing source access. Treat
equal-weight competition as a rendering hypothesis until pixels are inspected.
Do not invent a missing-handler defect or report a measured time saving.

For an authorized fix, compare the same content and viewport after changing the
structural competition; re-walk source access and continued reading. Fixing one
badge or one title wrap cannot establish closure of the declared whole-path
complaint. A diagram earns attention by explaining an actual relationship, not
by satisfying an image quota.

## Healthy control: necessary context and distinct route jobs

Same actor and content, with these supplied observations:

- The chronological library supports “what changed recently”; topic routes
  describe different questions and show matching article examples. Overlapping
  membership is explicitly explained where it affects the reader's choice.
- Each row exposes the title and the difference it explains; secondary metadata
  is available on demand. The article opens with the argument and its mechanism.
- A mechanism caption distinguishes observations from the author's explanatory
  illustration. A nearby source control leads to the original evidence.
- The follow-up states the next question, rather than repeating a generic CTA.
- An independently consumed figure repeats a necessary unit and comparison label.

Expected audit action: retain context that prevents a specific misreading,
including the figure's unit, comparison label and evidence boundary. Do not
flag overlapping topics or every repeated label as inherently redundant.
Explain how the routes support different choices; do not prescribe a palette,
grid, number of controls, or “one article per row” as the only valid design.
The packet supplies semantic controls only: actual pixels, clicks, freshness
and human aesthetic acceptance remain unverified until separately observed.

and public release. That repair had exercised bilingual view buttons, source
links, comparison selection and diagram navigation; layout and SEO checks also
passed. It reworded controls, removed a separate explanation band and repaired a
misleading diagram state, but viewing narration still repeated the selected
action. Those observations did not establish necessity of the remaining copy.

bilingual dek or summary metadata. It was not an article-body review or rewrite.

### Final revision candidates and preservation controls

The final source revisions provide candidate calibration, not user-endorsed
templates or proof that every sentence was approved by a human:

- The low-score-agent dek keeps only “只要仍能编辑代码，分数下降的子版本也可能成为有用的父版本。” Its expanded summary replaces the reading instruction with the separate Appendix F experiment: an agent removed special-token logging, bypassed the detector and scored higher while tool hallucinations remained unresolved. The intervention and unresolved defect now supply the relationship the old sentence merely announced.
- The replay study keeps its historical-replay mechanism in the short dek. The expanded summary reports 317 versus 550 discovery calls and 2931.0 versus 3587.1 ms mean solver time across six held-out datasets. It retains that one dataset drives the lower mean while the other five become slower, and that incumbent retention guarantees a non-decreasing replay mean only on the identical fixed history pool, not later online improvement. These are supplied reported results, not independently revalidated scientific claims.
- The kernel-study summary drops the abstract connection clause while retaining “随后训练出的模型是否更会发现算法，所提供的案例尚未测量。” That limitation changes what the reader can infer from a faster training kernel; its negative wording is not grounds for removal.
- The bilingual View controls remain, as does the key distinguishing the current viewing start from stored-but-unread dashed branches. Only the duplicate visible-outcome narration is removed. Keep author attribution, the cited edition and original-source access where they identify the evidence being read.

Expected healthy judgment: explain the concrete misreading prevented by each
retained item. Do not demand every result number in the initial card, rewrite
attributed originals, or classify every scientific limitation as producer
self-defense. A new candidate can remain partial despite semantic improvements;
current pixels, actual interactions and human reading value need their own
observations. The executing auditor makes these judgments; no bundled script
decides whether prose is AI Slop.

## Bounded replay and closure

Ask a fresh auditor, using the ordinary Skill entry, to handle the synthetic
failing and healthy route packets above:

1. State the actor's task and what evidence the packet can establish.
2. For the failing case, name the unresolved whole-path problem despite the
   passing local checks, with a concrete observation and next discriminating probe.
3. For the healthy case, identify context whose deletion would cause a concrete
   misunderstanding; do not manufacture a blanket minimalism violation.
4. Separate the packet-based conclusion from unobserved pixels, behavior and
   real-reader acceptance. Preserve audit-only authority unless a fix is requested.

An auditor who passes the first case solely because controls work misses the
failure. An auditor who deletes every caption in the second case overgeneralizes
it. Passing both is a bounded calibration observation, not proof of future
design quality or permission to restart an accepted frontend.

## Ancillary-copy release control

Use this synthetic pair for the existing necessity check's pre-release pause
point. The actor is choosing an article, understanding its mechanism, and
checking the cited edition. These observations describe supplied visible states;
they are not evidence that an auditor visited a live site.

**Failing packet:** a collection headed “Articles” adds “Published articles,
each with its own evidence and research scope.” Its source section adds “The
original materials, preserving their version information” immediately above
paper titles, edition labels and working source links. An opened footer
disclosure shows “Article / Release / Content digest” with opaque identifiers;
the same identifiers remain in the machine delivery manifest. A figure's
caption, nearby note and following paragraph each repeat that it is illustrative
and not a measured score. The default view and these expanded states are supplied;
the search-error state has not been inspected. Geometry checks pass.

Expected action: inspect each ancillary group for a concrete reader consequence.
The two producer descriptions add no selection or source-identification fact
beyond the surrounding content. Opaque build identities do not establish the
paper edition. Repetition alone does not decide the figure boundary: preserve
an explicit illustration label and any unique semantic limitation, then remove
unneeded repetitions. Do not grant release-ready while relevant state coverage
or necessity dispositions remain unresolved. Do not turn technical-identity
cleanup into deletion of machine provenance.

**Near-boundary healthy packet:** a source title includes an edition whose
results differ from a later revision, with an expandable section locator and
an “Open original” action. A schematic is labeled “Mechanism illustration”;
its dashed-node key explains which observations the policy cannot access. An
independently consumed result chart repeats its unit and comparison baseline.
The article says that a suggestive function name does not establish a speedup,
and that lack of validation is not evidence the result is wrong. A search error
offers an actionable retry; its accessible name survives the shorter helper.

Expected action: retain these distinctions where their absence changes the
reader's evidence, mechanism interpretation or next action. Do not treat a
negative scientific statement, version label, source locator or repeated unit
as producer scaffold based on wording alone. A concise illustration label is
sufficient only when it preserves the relevant truth boundary. Report the
supplied-state judgment separately from unobserved rendering and human value.
