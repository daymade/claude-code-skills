# Illustrated spreadsheets and fill-in grids

Use this bounded core-visual check for a rendered grid with reference pictures and
editable fields. Use the maintained spreadsheet owner for reads and authorized
edits; this reference does not create a second spreadsheet automation workflow.

## Bind the reader's task

The reader must recognize the example, understand the row's requirement and find
where to enter their answer. Inspect the target application's actual canvas at
the reader's viewport and zoom. Pair each screenshot with its observed target URL
or document identity, sheet, selected region and viewport. A descriptive screenshot
filename is not target-identity evidence.

## Reject the false green

An illustrated inventory grid was compressed until six complete rows fitted the
first screen. Portrait photos became narrow strips, stage names wrapped into
isolated characters, and the reader rejected it despite an independent PASS.
Having all images present and opening a full-size preview did not make the grid
readable.

The accepted correction enlarged the photos, sized their column to their aspect
ratio, widened labels, shortened on-grid requirements, and separated editable
fields from computed results. Fewer visible rows were a consequence of making
the examples usable. That case establishes no universal row height or row-count
target.

## Inspect a representative slice

- Include a portrait or otherwise awkward image, the longest category name and
  requirement, a row without a matching image, and the ordinary entry controls.
- At normal grid size, identify the pictured scene or action. Confirm a full-size
  preview shows the right original, but do not use it to excuse an unreadable
  thumbnail. Check natural aspect ratio, alignment and column whitespace.
- Read the phase/category, header and requirement as a recipient. Check for orphan
  characters, clipping, excessive wrapping and explanations occupying the working
  area. Essential conditions must remain discoverable when copy is shortened.
- Identify the editable quantities, status, source and notes, and distinguish
  formula cells. Check row and column boundaries, focus/selection and dropdowns.
- Check that the reference actually illustrates the requested stage/action:
  finished equipment does not demonstrate installation. Example imagery does not
  establish a customer's inventory or identity.

Style changes must preserve values, formulas, validations and image references
outside their authorized scope. Use the spreadsheet owner's independent readback
for that check; visual appearance alone cannot prove preservation.

For an ordinary text/number grid without images, do not impose photo-sized rows
or require a picture preview. Fit the audit to the actual content and task.

## Report and stop

Give concrete failing cells/regions and their user consequence. If the user rejects
a previously passing result, reassess the criterion against their latest task
before repeating it. Verify a disputed reviewer observation against the actual
image and target identity; do not convert an unverified reading into a new task.
After a correction, recheck the affected reading and preservation axes, reusing
unchanged evidence. Recipient acceptance remains separate from a reviewer PASS.
