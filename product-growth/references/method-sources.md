# Ground the selected method

Use each source for its stated question; retrieve current provider documentation
before relying on changing interfaces. Treat action cards and coverage maps as
an operational synthesis, not a proven universal growth formula.

- [Berezovsky, How to measure cohort retention](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/measuring-cohort-retention): define return events and cohort conventions; compare compatible sources and adapt cadence/eligibility.
- [Rachitsky, How to determine your activation metric](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-determine-your-activation): move from candidate value moments/associations to intervention testing. Respect source access; do not require a subscription to execute this Skill.
- [Kohavi et al., Controlled experiments on the web](https://ai.stanford.edu/~ronnyk/2009controlledExperimentsOnTheWebSurvey.pdf): plan evaluation criteria, power and randomization; consider uncertainty, practical value and maintenance costs.
- [Stripe, Subscription analytics](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/analytics): reconcile provider-specific recurring-revenue/customer/usage definitions, without treating billing defaults as universal accounting rules.
- [Holtz et al., Reducing Interference Bias in Online Marketplace Experiments Using Cluster Randomization](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2020.01157): inspect market interference/design tradeoffs; avoid assuming clustering or switching periods always cures contamination.
- [CHAOSS, Project Awareness](https://www.chaoss.community/kb/metrics-model-project-awareness/): distinguish visible attention from the broader results it may proxy; measure accepted work and ongoing value separately when observable.
