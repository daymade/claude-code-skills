# Check reasoning with synthetic examples

Treat all numbers/products here as invented teaching data, not customer cases,
benchmarks or observed growth results.

## Bound referrer claims

Given 1,000 search referrals among 1,250 listed referrals and 4,000 views, report
80% of listed referrals. Report 25% as a count ratio to total views only if periods
and definitions match; do not call it acquired users, successful users or incremental
demand. Keep unlisted/unattributed traffic unknown.

## Detect a channel-mix reversal

Given fully mature cohorts and identical return definitions:

| Period | High-intent retained / entrants | Broad retained / entrants |
|---|---:|---:|
| Earlier | 80 / 100 | 10 / 100 |
| Later | 18 / 20 | 24 / 180 |

Calculate overall retention 45% → 21%, but channel retention 80% → 90% and
10% → 13.33%. Describe both aggregate decline and mix shift. Do not infer product
changes caused either within-channel improvement or the aggregate decline.
Check acquisition intent and comparability before choosing an action.

## Separate recurring revenue, customers and costs

Given starting recurring revenue 100 units, expansion 30, contraction 10 and churn
10, calculate NRR 110% and GRR 80%; exclude new customers. Keep customer retention
unknown without customer counts. Inspect contribution if variable costs outgrow
revenue. Do not assume this bridge measures cash.

## Separate adoption and impact

Given a verified listing, copied integration and reproduced failed install,
conclude availability, source reuse and a specific barrier respectively. Repair
an authorized defect and read the public entry back. Keep default enablement,
unique users, accepted tasks and retention uplift unknown until observed.
Search original feedback/follow-ups without requiring owner interviews.

## Recognize scoped experimental evidence

Given a verified fixed-horizon random assignment, intact logging and otherwise
valid design with activation 2,000/10,000 control and 2,400/10,000 treatment,
calculate +4 percentage points and +20% relative lift. For independent Bernoulli
outcomes, calculate a simple unadjusted normal 95% difference interval of roughly
+2.85 to +5.15 points. Scope inference to tested treatment/population/window;
check longer-term value, costs and guardrails before shipping. Do not infer
retention lift from activation. Replace that simple interval for clustered or
repeatedly inspected data with a design-appropriate analysis.

## Retire an obsolete repair lead

Given an old failure report and a cached version-one instruction page, plus a
verified current version-two artifact that corrects that instruction, remove the
old instruction repair from the current backlog. Keep external task success and
growth impact unknown. If only old text is available, propose refreshing evidence
before deciding whether a repair is needed. If a fresh consumer observation still
reproduces the failure, investigate that current failure instead of dismissing it
because an earlier fix was published.
