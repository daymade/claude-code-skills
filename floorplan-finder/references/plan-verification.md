# Verify the drawing

Read this before excluding a candidate or calling it a match. Use visible image
evidence; do not treat OCR, a model description, or the site's text as the drawing.

## Establish image and orientation

Open the original at sufficient resolution to see labels, door gaps, windows,
and the compass. Keep the complete uncropped original, including its north arrow.
Record whether the arrow is visible; do not assume the top is north.

Locate the apartment's exterior-to-interior entrance first. Trace the wall opening
and the outside/inside spaces. Distinguish:

- **Door position:** which side or shoulder of the outline contains the opening.
- **Facing direction:** the direction outward through that opening, after orienting
  the plan by its compass and confirming the user's meaning.
- **Door swing:** the leaf/arc showing how it opens. A swing toward the east does
  not make a doorway on a north wall an east-facing entrance.

An entrance on a recessed shoulder can face north even when much of the apartment
extends farther north. Identify that local wall; do not judge from the outline's
overall bounding box. If the exterior context or compass is unclear, mark direction
unknown and ask a precise question rather than force an exclusion.

## Compare connected spaces

Walk from the entrance through the hall, living room, dining room, kitchen,
bedrooms, bathroom zones, and balconies. Record both relative positions and
connections. Room counts and area are filters; the adjacency and orientation
pattern distinguish similarly sized layouts.

| Feature | Inspect | Avoid inferring |
|---|---|---|
| Bedrooms | Enclosed rooms, doors, labels and window orientation | Every room-like box is a bedroom |
| Living/dining | Their positions and continuous connection | The site's 厅 count maps exactly to the user's wording |
| Kitchen | Relationship to dining, access and exterior opening | A side kitchen establishes a building number |
| Bathroom | Access from hall/bedrooms, serial zones, visible fixtures and source count | 卫生间 A/B means two independent bathrooms |
| Balconies | Connections, facing and shape; retain small service balconies | Every balcony must have been mentioned by the user |
| North–south through layout | Actual alignment/openings and connected living/dining spaces | A two-dimensional drawing proves measured ventilation |

If a site says one bathroom but labels two connected bathroom zones, retain the
candidate and flag the conflict. Serial wet/dry compartments are one possible
explanation, not an established fact without fixture or occupant evidence.
Likewise, verify two independently accessible bathroom suites when the user
requires one; do not excuse a clear mismatch as zoning.

## Preserve meaningful differences

Compare a compact feature table across plausible originals:

| Candidate | Bedrooms N/S and side | Living/dining side | Kitchen connection | Entrance | Bathroom evidence | Unresolved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Image observation | Image observation | Image observation | Compass + opening | Labels/access/fixtures | Specific distinction |
| B | Image observation | Image observation | Image observation | Compass + opening | Labels/access/fixtures | Specific distinction |

Keep a rotated display and a mirrored layout distinct. Rotation changes page
presentation; mirroring reverses left/right geometry and can change the actual
unit. Never mirror an image to manufacture a match. If an annotated/rotated copy
helps comparison, label it as a derivative and retain the original source.

Treat standard floors, ground floors, top floors, duplexes and renovated plans
as separate variants until evidence joins them. “Same building” does not settle
these differences. Ask the user about the cheapest decisive feature instead of
requiring a full set of new measurements.

## Reopen a rejected candidate

When a conclusion conflicts with a strong source or the user's correction,
return to the original image and the exact rejection reason. Recheck the compass,
door opening and functional room count independently. Withdraw the earlier
exclusion explicitly if its evidence fails; do not keep searching under it.
