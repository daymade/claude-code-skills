# Completeness Review Checklist

Verify the draft meets all requirements before delivery.

## Structure and Format
- All required sections present and ordered
- Headings match the format contract
- Length matches the reader's decision and requested format

## Evidence and Citations
- Every consequential factual claim has a citation
- Every exact number, date, and quotation has been checked in the original source
- Conflicting sources are explicitly noted
- Evidence table maps claims to sources
- Multiple domains that repeat one underlying record are grouped as one evidence family

## Content Quality
- Findings answer the research questions
- Recommendations are tied to evidence
- Limitations and uncertainty are documented
- Terminology is consistent
- Every load-bearing claim is supported, contradicted, or explicitly unknown
- Counter-evidence was sought for each major conclusion

## Final Checks
- Dates and time ranges are consistent
- No unsupported claims
- No duplicate or conflicting statements
- First-party records are not described as independent external validation
